Smarter supports

Take complete control over your supports with our new Support Painting feature. You can now customize exactly where supports are placed simply by brushing them directly onto your 3D model. Whether you want to fine-tune auto-generated structures or paint an entirely custom support pattern from scratch, this intuitive tool gives you absolute authority over your print's stability. Tree supports also got smarter with these new features: additional base for stability, smoothing and dynamic wall thickness. By combining them all, you can significantly reduce print times, save filament, and ensure cleaner surface finishes upon support removal.
